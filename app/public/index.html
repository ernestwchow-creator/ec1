About AI Estimates

When a food is not in our curated reference database, nutritional values are estimated by Claude, an AI model by Anthropic.

How it works

  • Food identification — Claude analyzes your photo to identify each food item and estimate portion size.
  • Nutritional estimation — For each food, Claude draws on its training data (which includes USDA, nutrition textbooks, and food science literature) to estimate oxalate, calcium, carbohydrates, fiber, and glycemic index per 100g.
  • Fallback only — These AI estimates are only used when the food is not found in our reference database, which contains curated values from published sources.

Accuracy

AI estimates are approximate. They are reliable enough for general guidance (e.g. "puff pastry is low-oxalate, high-carb") but may not match exact lab-measured values. For foods you eat regularly, you can correct the identification to a database food or scan the product's nutritional label for precise values.

What's in the reference database?

Our database contains ~95 foods with values sourced from the Oxalosis & Hyperoxaluria Foundation, USDA FoodData Central, and the University of Sydney GI Database. Foods in the database show source links instead of "AI estimated."
